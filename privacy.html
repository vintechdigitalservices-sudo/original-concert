Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how Throne Nation handles your information.


1. Data Collection

We collect basic information (such as name and contact details) during the ticket registration process. Successful registrations are stored as data-only records in our database under the tickets collection. Your uploaded portrait photo is never uploaded or stored in our database; it remains on your device and is used only in your browser for the purpose of generating your personalized attending flyer.


2. Purpose of Collection

The data we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Follow-up: To provide updates regarding the event.
  • Communications: To keep you informed about future Throne Nation events.
  • Community: To foster a connected community of worshipers and creatives.

3. Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.


4. Third Party Sharing

Throne Nation does not sell or trade your personal data to third parties.


5. Your Rights

You may request access to your data or ask for its removal from our records at any time.
